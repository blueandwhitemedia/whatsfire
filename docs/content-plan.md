## Linking rules
- Each article links up to its pillar and hub, across to two siblings, and to the calculator when numbers matter.
- Glossary entries link to the full article; the article links back.
- No links to fireinstitute.com for now (Jonathan, Nov 2). Where a topic overlaps with FIRE Institute's beginner topics, What's FIRE stays definitional and the two sites do not publish near-identical articles.

## Publishing
Two articles a week once the site is live. Each new article gets a page-1 study first (playbook in #37), and Jonathan approves the finished article.
