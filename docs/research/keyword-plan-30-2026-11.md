## Free verification procedure
1. Google Keyword Planner (US, last 12 months): collect actual search-volume **ranges**, if Ads setup allows. Log the access date and exported range; do not treat ad competition as SEO difficulty.
2. Ahrefs free Keyword Generator or Semrush free checker: sample 5 priority queries per day and record **actual** KD only if presented. Missing metric = “not available.”
3. Google Trends: compare 5 related phrases but report only normalized interest, not monthly searches.
4. Manually review top results for each tier-one keyword, noting institutional domains, match to intent, forums, fresh results, and SERP features.
5. Once search data exists, let Search Console impressions and clicks determine actual publishing order; re-check content cannibalization before adding duplicate URLs.
