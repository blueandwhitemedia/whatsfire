## Differentiation and positioning
**What's FIRE:** publish encyclopedic definitions and comparison articles for curiosity-led searches. Do not copy the same keyword targets from FIRE Institute, which owns the structured action challenge. Include cross-brand links where appropriate but keep pages genuinely distinct.


## Practical traffic-source investigation (free)
- Search each brand's name, 3 head keywords and 3 long-tail keywords in Google US search; log which competitor pages are visibly ranking, with collection date.
- Inspect public site architecture, landing-page CTA, case studies, newsletter signup, podcast channel and social links. Presence != share of actual traffic.
- Identify 3 content gaps by task intent (checklists, calculators, data, state-specific guidance where applicable) and build original assets.
- Use your own Search Console to validate first-party impressions and conversion results. Competitor visitor estimates, keyword rankings, ad spend and traffic shares require a provider dataset; **not acquired here**.
