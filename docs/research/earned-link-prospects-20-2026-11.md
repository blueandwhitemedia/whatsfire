## Four-week execution plan
1. Publish at least two original shareable resources with verified sources, useful data or calculations; do not repackage other publishers' writing.
2. Split targets into direct editorial relationships, association/public education resources, publisher submissions and community contributions. Check their current content and link policy before any pitch.
3. Send no more than five genuinely personalized pitches each week, mentioning why the specific audience benefits; offer a noncommercial version if appropriate.
4. Record date, asset, recipient, response, link/mention URL and follow-up; honor refusals without repeated requests.
5. Never use purchased link schemes, link exchanges, paid reviews presented as editorial mentions, fake testimonials or undisclosed affiliate placement.
