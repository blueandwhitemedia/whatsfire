## Operational recommendations
What's FIRE is an ad/affiliate informational publication; don't displace unbiased financial basics with products merely because payout is high. Respect overlaps with FIRE Institute and make educational content distinct.

Apply only when the site has enough quality traffic to satisfy program approvals, as Jonathan prefers. Verify signed publisher contract and actual conversion event, cookie/attribution terms, product exclusions, fees and required disclosures before activating links. Use `rel="sponsored"` on qualifying outbound links and show FTC disclosures. Recheck this catalog quarterly.
