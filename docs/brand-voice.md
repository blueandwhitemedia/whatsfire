# What's FIRE? brand voice (#12.11)

Draft for Jonathan's approval. Based on the brief and `docs/research/brand-social-copy-2026-11.md`.

## Who is speaking
A friendly guide who has read a lot about FIRE and explains it plainly. Not a guru, not a salesperson, not a financial adviser.

## Tone
- Warm, curious, plain. Like explaining it to a friend over coffee.
- Specific over vague: a real example with real numbers beats "many people".
- Honest about limits. FIRE is not for everyone and not guaranteed.
- Calm. No urgency, no hype, no "secret".

## Do
- Define every term the first time it appears.
- Use short sentences and everyday words. Write "use" rather than "utilize".
- Show the math in a small example, then link to the calculator.
- Say "it depends" and then say on what.
- Mention the Financial Disclaimer near money guidance.
- Use "you" and "we" sparingly; never speak for the reader's situation.

## Don't
- No em dashes. Use a comma, a period or a colon.
- No guarantees, promised returns or "you will retire by X".
- No invented statistics, testimonials or performance claims.
- No "game-changer", "unlock", "dive into", "journey", "in today's fast-paced world", "it's important to note", "let's explore", "navigate", "landscape", "delve".
- No fear or shame about money.
- No ranking one product above another because it pays more (affiliate rule from the brief).

## Words to use
financial independence, retire early, FIRE number, savings rate, withdrawal rate, lean, fat, coast, barista, glossary, plain words, example.

## Words to avoid
get rich, passive income secrets, guaranteed, easy, effortless, hack, risk-free, "financial freedom" as a promise.

## Lines already chosen
- Tagline: Financial independence starts with understanding it.
- One line: A friendly introduction to the FIRE movement, from saving and investing basics to designing a life with more choices.
- Call to action: Discover What FIRE Means

## Article shape
Title as a question or plain statement. One-sentence answer first. Then the explanation, one example, "what this does not mean", related articles, disclaimer line. About 800 to 1,500 words, no padding.
