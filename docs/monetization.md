# What's FIRE? monetization plan (#12.141)

Draft for Jonathan's approval. Docs only; nothing here changes the live site. All earnings figures are planning guesses, not forecasts, and nothing here activates an ad or an affiliate link.

## Principles
- Reader first. Basics stay unbiased; no product is placed ahead of a better free option because it pays more.
- No applications until the site has real traffic (Jonathan's rule; see research/affiliate-programs-2026-11.md).
- Every affiliate link uses rel="sponsored" and sits near a plain disclosure. Affiliate Disclosure page stays accurate.
- Never quote "up to" payouts as ours. Check each program's signed terms before activating.
- No links to or from fireinstitute.com by default (Jonathan, Nov 2).

## Which offer goes where
| Page type | Primary offer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Definition and types articles (what is lean FIRE, etc.) | Display ads only | Informational, low buying intent. No product pushes. |
| FIRE math articles and /fire-number-calculator | Display ads, one planning-tool mention (ProjectionLab) in a "next step" box | Financial Disclaimer link stays visible. |
| Investing basics hub | Display ads; investing platform links only after a program approves the site | Betterment and similar are unverified candidates. |
| Real life (budgeting, kids, healthcare) | Budgeting app mentions (Rocket Money, YNAB if invited) | Per-action payouts are small; fit matters more than payout. |
| /resources | Amazon book links (4.5% physical books, 24-hour cookie) | Prices and images only from the Amazon API; no links in emails or PDFs. |
| Glossary, About, legal, contact | No offers | Trust pages stay clean. |

## Expected earnings per visitor (planning guesses to check against real data)
- Display ads (AdSense first, a premium network once traffic qualifies): finance traffic is paid better than average, but page RPM is unknown until the site has served ads. Plan on measuring for 60 days before setting any target.
- Affiliate: expect well under 1 click per 100 visits on informational pages and low single-digit conversion after the click, so affiliate income stays small until there are comparison and tool pages. Treat it as a bonus on top of ads at first.
- Newsletter: no direct revenue early; its job is returning readers (and later, sponsor or affiliate sends). Opted-in only, via Resend from mail.whatsfire.com.

## Path to the first dollar
1. Launch with 15 priority-1 articles, calculator and glossary (content-plan.md).
2. Apply to AdSense once the site has enough original pages and some search traffic. Apply only after Jonathan approves.
3. Add Amazon Associates for the /resources book list once it has the required qualifying sales window in view; follow its disclosure line.
4. Apply to ProjectionLab and Rocket Money when there are pages where they truly fit and traffic to show.
5. Review in Search Console after 60 to 90 days; build comparison pages (the highest-intent queries) from what actually ranks.

## Metrics to log monthly (feeds the revenue log)
Sessions, ad RPM, affiliate clicks by page (sub IDs), conversions by program, revenue by source.
