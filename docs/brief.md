# What's FIRE? (whatsfire.com): site brief

Written 2026-11-19 for task #12.6, from the files in docs/research/ and the live code. For Jonathan's approval. Nothing here changes the live site.

## What the site is
A friendly, glossary-first introduction to the FIRE movement (Financial Independence, Retire Early). It answers the curiosity-led searches ("what is lean FIRE", "how does FIRE work") in plain words for people who have never heard the term. It is supported by display ads and affiliate links, not by a paid course.

## Who it is for
Curious, everyday earners who have just met the idea of FIRE and want to know what it means and whether it could work for them. Not experts, and not high-net-worth readers.

## How it differs from fireinstitute.com
FIRE Institute owns the structured challenge, the Kickstart and the membership path. What's FIRE explains and defines. The two must not publish near-identical keyword articles. Where a topic overlaps, What's FIRE stays encyclopedic and links across to FIRE Institute for the action steps; if a page must overlap, set a canonical or pick a different angle first.

## Content plan summary (full list: keyword-plan-30-2026-11.md)
Six clusters: Definition, Types (lean, fat, coast, barista, slow FI, expat), Comparisons, FIRE math, Real life (kids, mortgage, healthcare), Pitfalls. Publish the 15 priority-1 queries first. Volumes and difficulty are not measured; let Search Console decide order once data exists.

## Money
Display ads once traffic allows, plus affiliate links only where a program has approved the site (affiliate-programs-2026-11.md). No application until the site has real traffic. Use rel="sponsored" on affiliate links and keep the Affiliate Disclosure page accurate. Never present "up to" payouts as ours. Do not push higher-paying products ahead of unbiased basics.

## Rules the copy follows
- Educational only. No guarantees, no invented performance, no promised savings or returns.
- Keep the Financial Disclaimer link visible near calculators and any money guidance.
- No em dashes; plain, warm, specific wording; nothing that reads as AI-written.
- Email, if added, sends through Resend from mail.whatsfire.com (opted-in only), per the shared email rules.
